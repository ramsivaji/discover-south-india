🔥 Must-Eat Andhra Food

Taste the Fire of Andhra

Andhra cuisine is legendary — fiery, tangy, and soul-satisfying. Here are 8 iconic dishes you must try.

🍚

Pulihora

Tangy tamarind rice seasoned with peanuts, curry leaves, and mustard — a festival staple that explodes with flavor in every bite.

🌿

Gongura Pachadi

The signature Andhra chutney made from sorrel leaves. Tangy, spicy, and addictive — locals say a meal without gongura is incomplete.

🥞

Pesarattu

Crispy green moong dal dosa served with ginger chutney and upma — the iconic Andhra breakfast that fuels millions every morning.

🫓

Ragi Mudda

Rayalaseema's soul food — finger millet balls served with fiery natukodi (country chicken) or tangy pulusu curries.

🍲

Ulavacharu

Rich horse gram lentil soup slow-cooked with spices — the ultimate comfort food that warms your soul on rainy evenings.

🧆

Punugulu

Golden deep-fried rice batter fritters — crispy outside, fluffy inside. The perfect tea-time snack paired with coconut chutney.

🥙

Bobbatlu (Puri)

Sweet stuffed flatbread filled with chana dal and jaggery — a festive dessert that melts in your mouth with ghee.

🍗

Natu Kodi Pulusu

Country chicken curry cooked with tamarind and fiery red chilies — Andhra's most beloved non-veg dish, best with steamed rice.
